Phase 01 / capture

Direct-to-chip capture

Coolant runs across the hottest silicon, not the room. Heat never reaches the air, so the rack never throttles.

  • Micro-channel cold plates on CPU / GPU packages
  • Rack delta 85 °C → 32 °C at the manifold
  • No room-scale air conditioning load
01 Visual · macro rack interior · crimson emissive dies · fluid shimmer

Phase 02 / transport

High-velocity extraction

Sealed conduits pull the load away at 4.2 litres a second. Nothing evaporates, and nothing touches the electronics.

  • Closed-loop manifold, zero evaporation
  • Particle flow fields through translucent pipe
  • Indigo at inlet, crimson under load
02 Visual · waveguide manifold · particle streams · slow dolly back

Phase 03 / exchange

The exchange

Two loops share a wall and nothing else. Ninety-six percent of the heat crosses over; zero fluid crosses with it.

  • Counter-flow plate core, 95 %+ effectiveness
  • Crimson → blue on the process side
  • Municipal water leaves warm amber
03 Visual · heat exchanger macro · dual-loop colour contrast · no mixing

Phase 04 / reuse

District energy

The camera rises through the slab. Warm mains fan out under the street and light up the buildings above.

  • Subterranean mains glow amber outward
  • Housing, schools and civic centres on the loop
  • Once-wasted heat becomes zero-carbon warmth
